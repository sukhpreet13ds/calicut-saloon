Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 17-August-2026

At Calicut Men's Hair Beauty, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website and services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Book an appointment
  • Contact us through our website
  • Submit an enquiry or contact form
  • Request information about our services
  • Subscribe to promotional communications
  • Communicate with us by phone, email, WhatsApp, or other available channels

The information we may collect includes:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Appointment details
  • Service preferences
  • Messages or enquiries submitted through our website
  • Any other information you choose to provide

We may also automatically collect certain technical information when you visit our website, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and general website usage information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Schedule and manage appointments
  • Respond to enquiries and requests
  • Provide information about our salon and services
  • Confirm or remind you about appointments
  • Communicate with us regarding your bookings
  • Improve our website and customer experience
  • Process payments where applicable
  • Send promotional offers or updates when permitted
  • Maintain records for business and administrative purposes
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, or unauthorized activity

3. Appointment Information

When you book an appointment through our website or other communication channels, we may use the information you provide to manage your appointment and contact you regarding changes, confirmations, or reminders.

Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up to date.

4. Payments

If payments are available through our website, payment information may be processed by third-party payment providers.

We do not normally store complete credit or debit card details on our website. Payment information is handled according to the privacy and security practices of the applicable payment provider.

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, understand website usage, and provide a better browsing experience.

Cookies may help us remember certain preferences and understand how visitors interact with our website.

You may adjust your browser settings to disable or manage cookies. However, disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services such as:

  • Appointment or booking systems
  • Payment processors
  • Website analytics services
  • Communication platforms
  • Social media integrations
  • Hosting and security services

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you use through our website.

7. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share information with trusted service providers when necessary to operate our website, manage appointments, process payments, provide communications, or perform other legitimate business functions.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal proceedings, or to protect our rights, customers, property, or safety.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain business records, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

When information is no longer required, we may securely delete or dispose of it.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of certain information
  • Withdraw consent where applicable
  • Opt out of promotional communications
  • Ask questions about how your information is being used

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the details provided below.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are primarily intended for general customers and are not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent where such consent is required by applicable law.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, website, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
